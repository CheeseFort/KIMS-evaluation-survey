FUSION EVALUATION SURVEY

Fall 2026 EE Capstone Fusion Evaluation Survey

Thank you for participating

This fourth-year electrical engineering capstone project aims to help firefighters and other first responders detect people in low-visibility environments, such as smoke-filled buildings, by combining visible-light (RGB), infrared (IR), and millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar data. This survey will help us assess whether the combined images allow people to identify and count people as accurately and confidently as using either image type alone, helping us evaluate the system's potential value in emergency response.

Please complete the survey by yourself and only once. The survey time is being recorded, so please avoid leaving the page open.

All responses are anonymous and are submitted at the end of the survey. If you do not wish to finish the survey, you may leave at any time before submission and your answers will be discarded. If you leave or reload the page, you will not be able to continue the survey.
